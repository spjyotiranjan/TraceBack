## Re-capture and remaining differences

Recaptured 88 Details views and 32 claim views with the same fixture states, themes and widths as the baseline. Of these, 80 Details and 24 claim captures correspond to the 05 and 08 frame matrices; the remaining captures cover scrolled facts, fallback and handoff states. The browser asserts representative geometry and order rather than treating different content as a pixel regression.

- **05 Found/Owner** (`12080`, `14572`, `17044`, `19696`; `12210`, `14701`, `17182`, `19833`): Chromium measured the photo at 560 × 368 and preview at 560 × 184 on desktop, and 350 × 270 and 310 × 184 on phone, in both themes. The phone now shows the title above the photo, the map ahead of the description, and the claim action after the facts/contact. On desktop the eligible action precedes the facts. The shared navigation/background remains the project override. Actual report photo framing, Leaflet tiles/attribution and fixture text differ from the illustration. Owner actions remain grouped, but still flow to new lines when the available width or label demands it.
- **05 fallback/interaction** (`12954`, `13328`, `13538`, `13672`, `13809`, `13941`, `14049`, `14159` and their derivatives): the preview retains its bounds when media fails; error and retry UI use the application's copy. The compact discussion panel preserves reply/attachment state and a separately labeled text alternative. The scrolled composer captures show the active controls; some 05 device frames show the top of the report rather than the corresponding scroll position, so those are not claims of a pixel match. The details-components PDF is the reference for the composer and attachment controls. Guest discussion remains sign-in gated by the existing endpoint, rather than making a guest read-only feed as suggested in the checklist (no dedicated frame).
- **08 default and long title** (`39658`, `39948`, `40232`, `40522`; `39870`, `40155`, `40444`, `40729`): the 432 px desktop and full-width phone drawers now use a 72 px thumbnail, stacked privacy copy, a 156 px starting textarea and a divided action footer. The actual thumbnail/placeholder, border tokens and copy can differ from the export. Long titles wrap and the form body scrolls independently of the actions.
- **08 validation/duplicate/pending** (`39736`, `40024`, `40310`, `40598`; `39784`, `40071`, `40358`, `40645`; `39831`, `40117`, `40405`, `40691`): retain server-provided messages, linked field errors, a focused summary and a visible count, instead of substituting sample copy or removing feedback absent from the export. Success uses the existing Dashboard redirect and one-time status notice rather than a new drawer confirmation. Tests cover retryable failure and duplicate states separately.

## Verification scope

The loopback Razor/Chromium checks cover 1440 × 1040 and 390 × 844 in Light and Dark, the 895/896 px transition, 320 px and short viewport reflow, and native 200% Chromium zoom (720 CSS px at a 1440 px window). They exercise keyboard dismissal and returned focus, touch emulation, reduced-motion visual baselines, photo/map failures, owner/visitor/status gating, comment cursor recovery, replies, image attachment, and the real standalone/no-JS claim flow. Claim integration checks exercise authorization, antiforgery, validation, persistence failure, duplicate prevention and Dashboard handoff against disposable loopback MongoDB. No physical-phone keyboard, live storage or live map provider was tested.
