# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Campus members who need to report, find, or recover belongings; administrators who review claims and moderate reports.

## Product Purpose

Help campus members share lost and found reports, search reports, submit private ownership claims, and manage their activity. Administrators review claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The user confirmed that the current flows should be retained and the work is a UI improvement. Existing MVC routes, access rules, validation, map and image behavior, discussions, and claim handling are the product baseline. No existing external visual reference or additional brand commitment was identified.

## Evidence on Hand

The source of truth is the existing controllers, Razor views, models, static assets, and project documentation under `src/TraceBack` and `docs`.
